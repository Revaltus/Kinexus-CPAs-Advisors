## LinkedIn

A 12-person framing contractor does $3.2 million in revenue, but the insurance application still shows last year's lower number and an old payroll split. Nobody notices until the audit true-up lands months later.

That is a calendar problem, not a hard one. Our December compliance calendar for St George contractors lists what to date-stamp before January:

- Insurance and bonding reviews, started 60 to 90 days before renewal
- Contractor license and qualifier dates (renewal cycles are not annual)
- Auto-renewing equipment leases and their notice windows
- Utah entity renewals, with a reminder 30 days out
- A clean year-end WIP schedule for your surety

December works because equipment must be placed in service by December 31, and Q4 estimates, W-2s, and 1099s all land within weeks of the new year. Put a named owner next to every line, and January starts with clear dates instead of surprises.
