## LinkedIn

A St George design-build firm posts a $60,000 loss for 2025 and still keeps $21,600 out of its payroll tax bill. (Our worked example is hypothetical, but the mechanics are real.)

The firm documents $360,000 of qualified research expenses on a prefab wall panel system and applies the 6 percent alternative simplified method. It then elects on Form 6765 to apply the credit against employer payroll tax. Its Q2 2026 Social Security liability on Form 941 drops from $13,950 to zero, and Q3 drops by $7,650.

Contractors are often told that no income tax means no R&D credit. That advice is out of date. With under $5 million in gross receipts and first receipts within the last five tax years, you can offset up to $500,000 a year: $250,000 against Social Security and $250,000 against Medicare.

The catch is timing. The election has to be on a timely filed original return, not an amended one.

The post covers what counts as research in construction, what documentation to keep, and the quarterly Form 8974 steps: https://stgcpas.com/resources/pre-profit-contractors-can-still-cash-in-how-to-use-the-r-d
