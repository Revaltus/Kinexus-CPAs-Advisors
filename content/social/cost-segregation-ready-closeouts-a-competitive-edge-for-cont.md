## LinkedIn

A 24-unit apartment building in Washington County turns over with a 40-line schedule of values. The developer's cost segregation provider needs 400.

That gap is where the weeks of back-and-forth, higher study fees, and strained relationships come from. On a $6,000,000 depreciable building, reclassifying 25% to shorter-lived property puts $1,500,000 up for a first-year deduction at 100% bonus depreciation. That is roughly $555,000 of federal tax deferred for an owner at 37%. Deferred, not erased, and their CPA will model that. What they need from you is the raw material.

A cost-segregation-ready closeout package supplies it: an itemized schedule of values, change order scopes with written descriptions, subcontractor invoices, as-built drawings, and a site work breakdown. "Electrical: $412,000" forces the provider to guess. "CO 7: add 14 exterior wall packs and conduit run to east parking area" does not.

Contractors who build this in from preconstruction are the ones developers invite back. Our post covers the full checklist and how to set up your cost codes.
